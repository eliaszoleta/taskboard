Achiever Board is a free, browser-based kanban tool built for people who want to stay organized without learning a new system or paying a monthly subscription.

What is Achiever Board?

Achiever Board is an online kanban-style task manager that helps individuals and small teams organize their work into clear, visual columns — To Do, In Progress, Done, and Overdue (automatically flagged). No installation is needed. It runs entirely in your web browser.

Whether you're managing a personal project, tracking freelance work, or coordinating a small team, Achiever Board gives you a clear picture of what needs to happen and who's working on what — without the bloat of enterprise tools that cost more than they're worth.

Achiever Board comes in two versions. The Solo Board is completely free, requires no account, and stores your data in the cloud under a board name you choose. The Team Board adds multi-user accounts and a full suite of collaboration tools — task assignment with instant notifications, comments with real-time reply threads, 1-on-1 direct messages, a team announcement board, personal activity feed, date filters, real-time sync, and priority labels. Flat team rate — not per person — free forever for up to 2 users, with plans from $15/month for larger teams.

Why we built it

Most task management tools are either too simple (just a checklist) or too complex (project timelines, Gantt charts, resource allocation). There's a gap in the middle for people who want a real board with priorities, due dates, and filters — but nothing more complicated than that.

Achiever Board fills that gap. It's designed to be fast to set up, easy to use on day one, and genuinely useful without reading a manual. You open it, create a task, and get on with your day.
