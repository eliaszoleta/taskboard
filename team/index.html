The Free Task Management Tool Built for Real Teams

Everything you need to manage tasks — without paying a cent. No hidden fees, no feature paywalls, no expiring trials.

Free Kanban Board

Drag tasks across To Do, In Progress, and Done columns. Visualize your team's workload at a glance with a classic, intuitive kanban layout — 100% free.

Assign Tasks to Teammates

Create team accounts and assign tasks to specific people. Everyone sees what they're responsible for, reducing miscommunication and missed deadlines.

Due Date & Deadline Tracking

Set scheduled and due dates on every task. Overdue tasks are automatically flagged in a dedicated column so nothing slips through the cracks.

Priority Management

Tag tasks as High, Medium, or Low priority. Filter any column by priority to focus on what matters most right now — simple and effective.

File & Link Attachments

Attach files and resource links directly to tasks. Keep all project context in one place so your team never hunts through emails or chat for assets.

Real-Time Collaboration

Changes sync instantly for all team members. Leave comments on tasks, tag teammates, and get notified when tasks are updated — at zero cost.
