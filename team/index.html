Every Feature Your Team Actually Needs

All features are included on every plan — free or paid. No feature paywalls, no bait-and-switch. The only difference between plans is how many seats your workspace can hold.

Task Assignment + Instant Notifications

Assign any task to a specific teammate — they get notified immediately. No more "I didn't know that was mine." Everyone sees exactly what they're responsible for, every day.

Comments & Real-Time Threads

Leave comments directly on tasks. Teammates get notified the moment a comment is posted on their tasks. Keep all conversations in context — no more hunting through Slack for that one message.

Personal Activity Dashboard

Each user sees their own sidebar feed showing tasks assigned to them and recent activity. Filter the board by teammate to get a clear view of anyone's workload at a glance.

Due Dates & Overdue Tracking

Set scheduled and due dates on every task. Overdue tasks are automatically moved to a dedicated Overdue column — so nothing slips and no one misses a deadline.

Priority Management

Mark tasks as High, Medium, or Low priority. Filter any column by priority so your team always knows what to focus on, not just what's piling up.

File & Link Attachments

Attach files and resource links directly to tasks. All project context lives inside the task — no more chasing files across email threads, shared drives, or chat history.
