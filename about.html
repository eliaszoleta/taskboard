About

Task management that gets out of your way

AchieverBoard is a free, browser-based kanban tool built for people who want to stay organized without learning a new system or paying a monthly subscription.

What is AchieverBoard?

AchieverBoard is an online kanban-style task manager that helps individuals and small teams organize their work into clear, visual columns — To Do, In Progress, In Review, and Done. No installation is needed. It runs entirely in your web browser.

Whether you're managing a personal project, tracking freelance work, or coordinating a small team, AchieverBoard gives you a clear picture of what needs to happen and who's working on what — without the bloat of enterprise tools that cost more than they're worth.

AchieverBoard comes in two versions. The Solo Board is completely free, requires no account, and stores your data in the cloud under a board name you choose. The Team Board adds multi-user accounts, task assignment, real-time collaboration, and notifications — designed for teams that need more than a solo setup.

Why we built it

Most task management tools are either too simple (just a checklist) or too complex (project timelines, Gantt charts, resource allocation). There's a gap in the middle for people who want a real board with priorities, due dates, and filters — but nothing more complicated than that.

AchieverBoard fills that gap. It's designed to be fast to set up, easy to use on day one, and genuinely useful without reading a manual. You open it, create a task, and get on with your day.
